How memories are found

{{template "tip-open" "What is an embedding model?"}}An embedding model is a small, cheap AI model that turns a sentence into numbers that stand for its meaning. With it, a search for “what do I eat?” also finds “I am vegetarian”, although no word is the same. Most providers offer one, for example text-embedding-3-small.{{template "tip-close"}}

Memory always works. With an embedding model it also finds things when you use different words.
