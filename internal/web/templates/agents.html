Your agents

An agent is a saved worker with a job, its own tools and a to-do list. It runs in the background, on a schedule or when asked, and starts fresh every run.

{{template "i-bulb"}}How do agents work?
  1. 1Create it

    Give it a name and describe its job in plain words. Pick the tools it may use.

  2. 2Give it work

    Add tasks to its to-do list, or let it wake up on a schedule. It works without you.

  3. 3Talk to it from a chat

    Connect it with the Agents button in any chat, then just ask: “ask Inbox helper to reply to Sam”.
