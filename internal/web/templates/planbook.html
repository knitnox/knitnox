Who may use my Plan Book

You always can. You decide whether the AI and your agents may, too.

{{if .On}}On The AI can read and change your tasks{{else}}Off Only you can see your tasks{{end}}
  • In your own chats the AI can list, add, change and tick off tasks when you ask.
  • In groups the Plan Book is never used. To add something said in a group, press Take to my chat under the message.
  • Agents get nothing unless you allow one: open the agent, then Setup → “Which tools may it use?”. You choose read-only or full access.
  • Nothing is lost by mistake. A deleted task waits in the bin for {{.BinDays}} days, and each task keeps a history of who changed it.

How the Plan Book works

Your own to-do list. You, the AI in your chats and the agents you allow all work on the same list.

  1. 1Write it down

    Type a task in the box on the Tasks tab and choose when it is due. Or tell the AI: “remind me to renew my passport next week”.

  2. 2Group it

    Put tasks that belong together into a project. Each project shows how far along it is.

  3. 3Tick it off

    Tap the circle when a task is done. Wrong tap? Every change has an Undo button.

The row of seven days above your tasks shows the week ahead. The small number on a day is how many tasks are due then; tap a day to see only those. It is private to you and never used in group chats. Read the guide
