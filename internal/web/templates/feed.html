Who may post to my feed

You always can. You decide whether the AI and your agents may, too.

{{if .On}}On The AI in your chats can post to your feed{{else}}Off The AI in your chats cannot post{{end}}
  • In your own chats the AI posts when you ask it to, and can list or delete posts for you.
  • In groups the feed is never used. To post something said in a group, press Take to my chat under the message.
  • What the AI and agents post is always private. Only you can share a post{{if .PublicOn}} with everyone{{end}}, and they never read what other people shared.

How the Feed works

One page of short posts, newest first, so you see at a glance what happened.

  1. 1Short posts

    A post is a few lines. A longer report, a chart, a drawn diagram, a picture, a recording or a video can sit with it.

  2. 2Your helpers post too

    The AI in your chats and your agents post what they found, so you do not have to open each one.

  3. 3You stay in charge

    Posts are private until you share them. Change or delete any post; deleted ones wait in the bin ({{.BinCount}}) for {{.BinDays}} days.
